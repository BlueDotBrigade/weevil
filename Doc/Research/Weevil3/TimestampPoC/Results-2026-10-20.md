## Decision rule

Keep the column, unless parsing on demand adds under 5 s to opening a 20 GB log and keeps an `@Elapsed` filter under 10 s. Insights run on one thread today.

| Way | Extra at open | `@Elapsed` filter, all records | Within the rule? | Reading every timestamp, 16 threads |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plugin parse | +86.5 s | 71.2 s | No | 11.1 s |
| Span parse | +48.1 s | 39.4 s | No | 5.8 s |
| Fast parse | +5.2 s | 3.8 s | No | 0.6 s |

**Answer:** keep the column. No way of parsing on demand is within the rule.

All ways gave the same answers.
